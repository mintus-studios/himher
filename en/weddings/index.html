So glad you're here

We're Brittany and Diet. Brittany sings, Diet plays guitar and harmonica, and we both sing. Weddings are our favorite gigs, because hardly any day has so many moments that call for music.

We'll be there for your whole day, with up to 4 hours of live music, our own sound equipment and songs your guests will sing along to.

Your day with us

  1. Ceremony

    Sung live, not played from a speaker: for your walk down the aisle and the moments that matter.

  2. Cocktail hour

    Relaxed music while you take photos and your guests mingle.

  3. First dance

    Your song, sung live. Have a special song? We'll learn it for you.

  4. Reception

    Then it's time to dance: sing-along covers, from classics to today's hits.

Frequently asked questions

How long do you play?

Up to 4 hours, spread across your ceremony, cocktail hour and reception as you like.

Do you bring your own sound equipment?

Yes, we bring our own sound equipment.

Can you play our first-dance song?

Yes. Tell us your song, and we'll learn it for you.

Where do you play?

When we're in the US, we're based in Bergen County, New Jersey, and play across North Jersey and New York City. Further away? Just ask. In Germany, we play in Mainz and the Frankfurt Rhine-Main region.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your wedding: length, schedule and travel. Get in touch and we'll send you a quote.
